## Day 1 — Run AI on Your Own Laptop

**Hook:** What if you could run AI without paying a monthly subscription?

**Script:**

"Most people think using AI means sending every question and document to the cloud.

But there is another option.

You can download an open-source language model and run it locally on your laptop.

Tools like Ollama make this possible.

Once the model is downloaded, you can run supported models without an internet connection.

Imagine summarising internal notes, drafting content and experimenting with AI locally.

There are hardware limitations, and local models aren't always as capable as premium cloud models.

But for experimentation and privacy-conscious workflows, this is worth exploring.

AI doesn't always need a monthly subscription."

**Visual direction:** Laptop → Ollama → local model → sample prompt → generated response.

**On-screen text:** Local AI · No subscription required · Offline after download

**Caption:** AI doesn't always need to live in the cloud. Local LLMs are making AI experimentation more accessible. Would you run AI locally?

**CTA keyword:** LAPTOP

## Day 2 — The Two-Hour AI Work Test

**Hook:** Can AI actually reduce two hours of enterprise work?

**Script:**

"I wanted to test AI against three real marketing operations tasks.

First: campaign communication. AI helped structure the draft.

Second: performance reporting. AI helped identify patterns and prepare an executive summary.

Third: operational tracking. AI generated a tracker structure, but formulas and business logic still needed validation.

Here is my takeaway.

AI is excellent at accelerating structured work.

But business context, data accuracy and final accountability still belong to humans.

The goal isn't to replace professional judgement.

It's to give professionals more time to use it."

**Visual direction:** Three-task checklist, AI outputs, validation screen. Use a real test and disclose the actual result; do not present this script as a completed benchmark if it has not been run.

**On-screen text:** 3 tasks · AI assistance · Human validation

**Caption:** The real AI productivity test isn't how fast it generates text. It's whether the final output is usable.

**CTA keyword:** TEST

## Day 3 — Fix Long AI Conversations

**Hook:** Your AI project doesn't need one endless conversation.

**Script:**

"One mistake I see when people work with AI is keeping everything inside one enormous chat.

Eventually, finding decisions becomes difficult and context gets messy.

My approach is context engineering.

First, summarise the objective.

Second, preserve important decisions, constraints and outstanding tasks.

Third, move that context into a fresh conversation.

Fourth, verify that the new conversation understands the project.

This is especially useful when building agents, automations and complex technical projects.

Don't just write better prompts.

Manage your context better."

**Visual direction:** Long conversation → project summary → new conversation → validated continuation.

**On-screen text:** Compress · Preserve decisions · Restart · Validate

**Caption:** Context engineering is an underrated professional AI skill.

**CTA keyword:** CONTEXT

## Day 7 — Stop Paying for AI Tools You Don't Need

**Hook:** More AI subscriptions don't make you more productive.

**Script:**

"I believe in a zero-budget-first approach to AI adoption.

Before buying another tool, ask four questions.

Do I already have something that performs this task?

Have I actually used this subscription recently?

Does the paid version deliver measurable value?

Can a free alternative solve the same problem?

My rule is simple.

One primary AI assistant.

A small number of specialised tools.

And a clear reason for every subscription.

The objective is business value, not collecting AI logos."

**Visual direction:** Tool inventory → usage audit → free-tier alternatives → simplified stack.

**On-screen text:** Audit · Consolidate · Validate ROI · Reduce waste

**Caption:** AI adoption should be measured by outcomes, not subscription count.

**CTA keyword:** AUDIT
